# Sources

Every file in `data/` points back to an original page, video, or public register. This page is the human index: who each source is, why it is in the set, and what it is good for.

Counts below are files in `catalog/documents.jsonl` for talks, podcasts, and stories. For company tables they are rows inside the JSON shards.

## Talks and interviews (YouTube and adjacent writing)

`sales-scripter` | 790 files | en

## Founder stories

### Starter Story

`starter-story` | 2,219 files | en

Founder write-ups of real businesses: how the idea appeared, what it cost, what it earns, which channel worked. The structured metadata is often empty; the body text is where the numbers live. Original interviews remain copyright Starter Story; they are here for research and pattern analysis, with a link back to each page.

Topics: bootstrapped, case-studies, revenue. Origin: https://www.starterstory.com.

## Company and fund records

### Company events

`events` | 22,021 rows

A timeline of public signals (YC batch, recognition, and similar) keyed to company ids. Join to yc-companies and sec-edgar.

Derived from public records.

### Fund holdings inferred from Form D

`fund-holdings` | 30,635 rows

Attempt to roll Form D filings up to funds and their recent deals. Many rows still have unresolved names; treat as a raw join table, not a finished Crunchbase.

Origin: https://www.sec.gov/search-filings. Derived from US government public records.

### Y Combinator companies

`yc-companies` | 17,887 rows

Public YC directory snapshot: name, batch, location, team size, tags, status. The cleanest structured company table in the set.

Origin: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies. Public directory. Facts about companies; YC owns the presentation.

### SEC EDGAR (Form D and related)

`sec-edgar` | 22,602 rows

US private placement filings. Amounts, dates, and exemption types for companies raising under Regulation D.

Origin: https://www.sec.gov/search-filings. US government public records.
