# Notice

Two layers of rights sit on this repository. Mixing them up is how dataset
repos get taken down, so they are spelled out here.

## What VMSTE licenses

The files we wrote: the `catalog/` index, this documentation, the example
code, and the folder layout that groups sessions together.

Those are offered under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). Attribution: cite this repository
as `VMSTE/startup-data`.

## What still belongs to original authors

YouTube and podcast transcripts in `data/transcripts/`, founder write-ups
in `data/founder-stories/` (Starter Story interviews), and company pages
or news headlines copied into `data/companies/` when the source is not a
government register.

Each of those files keeps a link to the original URL. We host the text for
research and pattern analysis: finding repeated strategies, comparing how
founders describe the same motion, joining a talk to a public filing.

That is not a claim that we own the interviews. If you are the author or
rights holder and want a file removed, open a takedown issue. We will drop
the file and its catalog row.

## Public records

These slices are public records or open government data, redistributed as
facts about companies:

- Y Combinator public directory (`yc-companies`)
- US SEC EDGAR / Form D (`sec-edgar`, `fund-holdings`, parts of `events`)
- UK Companies House (`companies-house`)

`fund-holdings` is derived from Form D. Many rows have unresolved names.
That is a quality issue, not a rights issue.

## What we did not publish

Full books and ebook dumps that were in the local cache. Those are still
under copyright, and a research corpus does not need the complete text of
*Beyond Order* sitting next to a YC talk.
