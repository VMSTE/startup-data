   Klarna has successfully built an AI capable of doing the work of 700 customer service reps, resulting in significant cost savings. The AI acts as a co-pilot for customer service agents, resolving disputes and providing decision support. The implementation of AI has reduced resolution time from 14 minutes to 2 minutes, leading to higher customer satisfaction. In the marketing division, AI is used for translation and copywriting, resulting in increased productivity and cost reduction. Sebastian Siemiatkowski, CEO of Klarna, discusses the use of AI in employee engagement and customer surveys. Klarna built an internal AI interviewer to gather employee feedback, which provided more interesting and insightful outcomes compared to standard surveys. They also found success in using AI to survey customers and synthesize information. However, Siemiatkowski acknowledges that AI is not yet capable of replacing human managers or the creativity of humans in tasks like copywriting. He also talks about Klarna's use of generative AI for image creation and the future vision of a shopping assistant that helps users navigate different sites and make informed purchasing decisions.
