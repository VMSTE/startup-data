  B2B Cold calling success! Ryan Reisert, Brand Ambassador @Cognism goes through
  the funnel above the funnel. A funnel which ensures that sales reps don't try
  to sell your prospect on your product/service immediately, but ascertain
  whether the prospect is both contactable and the right person in the company
  to speak to. 


  Ryan goes through the problem sales reps have when cold calling and takes the
  Chet Holmes buyer pyramid and uses it to create the funnel above the funnel,
  focusing on build awareness, gathering information and ultimately, booking a
  meeting.
