  Why You Need to Qualify Referrals Before Sales Calls


  Remember, never assume, that just because someone's been referred to you, they
  are ready to buy.


  Focus on detaching yourself from the sales, and instead ask your
  referral  qualifying questions to see if there's a sales to be made. 


  Can you truly help that person? Or would it be wiser and healthier for your
  business not to make a sale?


  The average salesperson mindset is: Because they've been referred by X, they
  are ready to buy my solution! 


  The 7th figure salesperson mindset:  They are aware that without a clear
  understanding of their prospect's problems, they can't come up with a genuine
  solution. They will focus on finding the problem, and offering their solution.


  That way the sale doesn't feel like a sale, but more so like a solution to the
  prospect's problem!
