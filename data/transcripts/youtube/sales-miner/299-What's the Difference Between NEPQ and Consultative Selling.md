  What is more effective between the two types of selling - NEPQ™ and consultative selling?

  Consultative selling allows you to  find out your prospect’s needs, but it’s not good enough to get you a competitive advantage to make a difference in your sales outcomes. 

  NEPQ™, on the other hand, helps salespeople address what people WANT. You move people to persuade themselves to buy instead of you trying to do it for them.
