description: >-
  If you’ve been taught that you need to pitch and present your product or
  service to your prospect 50% of the time, then you need to STOP! 


  Why? Because prospects only need to hear far less than what you think they
  need to know. 


  An effective presentation does not talk about why a product or service is the
  best, instead it presents back the problems and challenges prospects told you
  during the discovery part of the call and how your solutions can help them get
  what they want.
