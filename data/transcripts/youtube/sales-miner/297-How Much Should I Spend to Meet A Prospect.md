description: |-
  Sales expert, Jeremy Miner, answers the burning question, "How much should I spend to meet a prospect?"

  Before you make any type of trip whether it's in car or maybe you're in more of a complex selling environment you sell B2B and they're asking you to make a flight to present in front of a boardroom...

  Before you make that trip you have to get smaller commitments from the prospect.

  You should not commit your valuable time and resources because there are too many obstacles in
  the way over which you have zero control.

  Before you travel to that prospect... You need to find a way to remove those other obstacles.
  If you're able to remove them then you can go. But removing those obstacles will make it much easier for you to get a commitment once you're meeting the prospect face to face.
