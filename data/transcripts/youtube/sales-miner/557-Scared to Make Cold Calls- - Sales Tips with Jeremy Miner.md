  Right, so cold calling has been around for a few decades now. 


  The question is: Do traditional cold calling techniques still work in today's
  new economy?


  The simple answer is: No


  95% of salespeople would start a cold call with the generic: 'Hi, My name is
  ... I am with xyz company and we do...' 


  But what's wrong with that you might ask? Well, first of all, you're talking
  to someone that doesn't know anything about you, therefore there's no
  established trust. If you open up the conversation focusing on what you sell,
  most likely the prospect will either say they're not interested, find other
  excuse or worse, hang up on you!
