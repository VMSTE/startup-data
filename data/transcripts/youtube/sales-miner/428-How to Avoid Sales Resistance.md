  Are you getting a lot of sales resistance from your prospects? 


  We’re always being sold to and so it’s no surprise that sales prospects will
  resist and object as much as they could. Prospects are especially triggered if
  they feel like you’re just one of those product pushers seeking to gain from
  them. 


  But, if you start to think like a problem finder and problem solver who is
  actually there for them, as the expert who has the answer to whatever problems
  they have, you’ll find less resistance and they’ll want to engage with you
  even more. 


  I have seen so many salespeople successfully overcome the resistance just by
  asking the right questions towards their prospects. Questions that disarm and
  encourage more engagement therefore leading to closed deals time and again.
