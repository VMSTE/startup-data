  How can we help someone feel joy, not burden, when they decide to become
  Catholic?


  In episode 16 of the Upstream Podcast, Patrick Lencioni is joined by Father
  Austin Gilstrap and Father Michael Fye to discuss how the Catholic Church can
  better welcome people who are becoming Catholic. They explore the importance
  of joy, hospitality, and personal relationships in the OCIA process. Together,
  they share practical ways to help converts feel celebrated, supported, and
  deeply connected to the Church.


  Topics explored in this episode: 


  (00:00) The Invitation to Catholicism

  * The growing interest in the Catholic Church. 


  (06:16) Seeing Conversion as Life-Changing

  * Father Fye shares how priests must stay close to the excitement of those
  entering the Church.


  (11:06) Hospitality and Individualized Paths

  * Father Gilstrap stresses the role of hospitality and explains how his parish
  personalizes the journey for each convert.


  (20:03) Relational Evangelization

  * How ongoing opportunities, one-on-one meetings, and relationships are
  central to OCIA.


  (25:48) Teaching, Mentoring, and Accompaniment

  * The priests share how volunteers, sponsors, and priests can best support
  those becoming Catholic.
