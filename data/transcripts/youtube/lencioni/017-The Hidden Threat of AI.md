  AI can be incredibly helpful — from language tools that support learning, to
  innovations that cure disease, to technology that increases productivity. But
  not all uses of AI are created equal.


  There is a growing trend of companies using technology to replace people
  simply because it cuts costs. When innovation is separated from ethics,
  purpose, and the dignity of work, the consequences ripple through the entire
  economy. Fewer jobs mean fewer customers. Fewer opportunities mean a weaker,
  less connected society.


  Just because something is possible doesn’t mean it’s good.


  This raises a crucial question for leaders, employees, and consumers:

  Are our decisions about AI serving the greater good, or just short-term
  convenience and profit?
