  How would your workplace culture change if love and service were driving

  In episode 250 of At The Table, Pat and Cody introduce the concepts of BaaM
  (Business as a Ministry) and WaaM (Work as a Ministry). They explore how
  viewing business and work through the lens of service and love can create
  healthier workplaces and stronger organizations. Whether you’re leading a
  public company or managing a small team, this conversation will invite how you
  think about purpose, culture, and results.


  Topics explored in this episode: 


  (0:32) Business as a Ministry (BaaM) 

  * How businesses can be seen as a way to serve others with love, going beyond
  just the transactional goal of making money


  (3:10) The Case for Love in Business

  * Tim Sanders’ book Love Is the Killer App.

  * How fear-based businesses struggle while love-centered cultures thrive.

  * The challenge of applying BaaM in public companies.


  (5:48) Work as a Ministry (Waam)

  * WaaM can be useful for employees in less purpose-driven organizations.


  (8:07) Ministry Doesn’t Sacrifice Results

  * John Gordon’s belief that love fuels high performance.

  * Love-driven work produces results, but should be pursued because it’s right.
