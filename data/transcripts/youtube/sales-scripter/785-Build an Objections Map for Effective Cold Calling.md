  mini stop sign that the prospect will hold up to try to bring the call to an
  end.  If we can agree that objections are likely to happen, then we can agree
  that being prepared for the most common objections can help to create
  effective cold calling.\r

  \r

  Creating an Objections Map\r

  In order to help be prepared for objections, it can help to have a tool or
  guide that outlines all of the most common or anticipated objections that you
  are likely to get while cold calling for the product or service that you are
  selling.  And to be most useful, this list of objections will also include the
  best responses that you can use to respond to those objections.  This type of
  tool or guide can be referred to as an objections map.\r
