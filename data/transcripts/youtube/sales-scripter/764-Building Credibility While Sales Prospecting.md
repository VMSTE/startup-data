description: >-
  There are two different times when it is important to build credibility while
  performing sales prospecting --  1) when dealing with gatekeepers and 2) when
  trying to get the prospect to move forward.


  Dealing with Gatekeepers


  The gatekeeper will typically be responsible for keeping out pesky sales
  people. As a result, when they answer an incoming call, the first thing they
  will try to determine is whether the person calling is a friend or a foe. A
  friend would be somebody that is already working with the company in some way
  and a foe would be an outsider trying to work their way in.


  Once the gatekeeper has identified that you are a foe, which you most likely
  are if you are sales prospecting, he or she will immediately become more
  difficult and try to keep you out. With that being the case, it can help to do
  everything that you can to avoid giving off the image that you are a foe.
