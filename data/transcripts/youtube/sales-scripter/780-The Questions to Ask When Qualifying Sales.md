  our ability to qualify the opportunities that we work on.  This is important
  because time is limited and valuable, so we must optimize our time management
  by working only on deals that are real in terms of interest, budget, and
  authority to buy.\r

  \r

  As a sales person talking to a prospect, you will typically know why they
  should buy from you as you know your products features and benefits inside
  out.  But in order to classify the opportunity as qualified, it is critical
  for the customer to know why they would buy from you or one of your
  competitors.  Instead of telling them why the should change, ask them to tell
  you why they are looking to change and the answer will tell you a lot in terms
  of the quality of the opportunity.  More at
