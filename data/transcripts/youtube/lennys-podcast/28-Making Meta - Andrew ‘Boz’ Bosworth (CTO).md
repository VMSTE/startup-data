  officer at Meta and head of Reality Labs, the company’s augmented

  Timeline, Privacy, and more. Before Reality Labs, he ran the Ads and Business

  analytics, and design, taking annual revenue from $4 billion to $40 billion in
  five years. Andrew currently leads Meta’s efforts in AR, VR, AI, and consumer
