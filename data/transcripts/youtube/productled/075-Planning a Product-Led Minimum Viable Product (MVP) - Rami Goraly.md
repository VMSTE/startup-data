description: |-
  Minimum viable products (MVP) allows product-led companies to test their product with customers and gather feedback without investing too much time, money, and resources. But how can you optimize your MVP planning, such as determining the ideal time to release it?

  Rami Goraly, CEO at OnceHub, recommends launching an MVP during the early stages of the market before early adopters lose interest. He emphasizes the importance of differentiating features and table stakes and cites OnceHub's entry into the online meeting scheduling market as an example of a well-timed MVP launch.

  || Key Takeaways ||
  • Understanding the difference between differentiators and table stakes is crucial when planning MVP.
  • Table stakes are essential features for a product, like reminders or cancellations.
  • The ideal window for introducing an MVP depends on the adoption curve and can vary between markets
