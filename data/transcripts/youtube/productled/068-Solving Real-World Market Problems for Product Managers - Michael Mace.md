  Michael Mace, VP Marketing Strategy at UserTesting, shares his expertise on market sizing, defining, and dominating in a cost-effective way.   || Key Takeaways ||
  • Conduct ongoing market research and product testing to ensure the accuracy of your data for decision-making purposes.
  • Gain valuable insights into customer preferences and needs through interviews, as quantitative surveys have limitations.
  • Prioritize solutions rather than just features, and remain mindful of market saturation.
  • Pay attention to the adoption curve and be cautious of subtle declines in growth rates, as they may serve as warning signs.
