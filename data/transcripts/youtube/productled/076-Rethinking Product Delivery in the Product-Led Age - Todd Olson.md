  In today's fast-paced business landscape, being product-led is crucial for success. However, simply having a great product is no longer enough.

  Todd Olson, CEO and Founder at Pendo, discusses how to rethink product delivery in the age of being product-led, focusing on decomposing different practices to remain competitive. He emphasizes the importance of reconsidering the way products are developed and launched, including using feature flags and beta releases to enable faster development and better customer adoption.

  || Key Takeaways ||
  Product-led approach puts product at the center of an organization.
  Rethinking product delivery requires rethinking product development.
  Feature flags allow for decoupling of engineering speed and customer adoption.
  Beta releases are useful for learning and defining release stages.
  Employees should be able to see releases for catch issues and learning.
