description: >-
  User adoption, otherwise known as onboarding, is a process where new users
  adopt a product (or service) and commit to using it. They’ve left their
  previous product behind and have actively decided to adopt a new system that
  is much more efficient and effective at helping them achieve their goal.


  Companies with high user adoption rates tend to see more users adopting the
  product than leaving it. If a company has a relatively low user adoption rate,
  it indicates that many users are adopting the product short-term and then
  jumping ship.


  So, how can you make sure more users are sticking with your product rather
  than abandoning it after signing-up?


  In this lesson from Ruairi Galavan, Senior Manager, product education at
  Intercom, you’ll learn 7 lessons learned from onboarding 30,000+ paying
  customers.

  1. What is product education?
