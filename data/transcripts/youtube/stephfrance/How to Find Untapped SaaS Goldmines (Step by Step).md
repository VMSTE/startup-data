  A practical framework to spot overlooked SaaS niches using real demand data
  and feature “unbundling.” We start from problem maps, validate search
  interest, identify big suite vendors, break down their feature sets, and
  isolate one‑feature products that can stand alone—then niche by industry for
  faster traction.


  Who this helps: indie hackers, solo founders, bootstrappers, no‑code/AI
  builders looking for B2B/B2C SaaS ideas with evidence of demand and clear
  positioning.


  What you’ll see, step by step:

  • Pick a market using two mind maps: the B2B Evergreen Problem Map and the B2C
  Core Markets Map (health, wealth, relationships).

  • Validate demand with Google Trends + Keywords Everywhere (search volume +
  CPC). Example metrics shown in the video:
    – “ATS software” ~15,000/mo (growing)
    – Employee recognition software ~4,000/mo (growing)
    – Application screening ~4,000/mo (growing)
    – PTO tracking (solid CPC ≈ $10)
    – Skill assessment software (lower volume but rising)

  size using Similarweb (example: HR suite with ~15M monthly visits).

  • Unbundle features with prompts (run in Claude) to extract simple,
  one‑feature SaaS candidates: employee satisfaction, salary benchmarking, PTO
  tracking, onboarding, interview scheduling, skill assessment, application
  screening, employee recognition, time tracking.

  • Niche down by industry with a second prompt to reach “category of one.”
    – PTO tracking → healthcare (complex shifts, on‑call, coverage rules)
    – Employee satisfaction → childcare (regulatory/staffing specifics)
  • Growth path: start vertical (industry‑specific), then expand horizontally
  (more industries) or vertically (more features) later.

  Claude (for market/feature analysis), plus problem maps to focus your
