  DESCRIPTION:


  A full walkthrough of the mental model for launching a SaaS product that
  actually acquires users. This video is structured around a clear step-by-step
  process: identifying growing or stable markets, researching user pain points,
  evaluating current solutions and competitors, and selecting your go-to-market
  strategy through either replication, differentiation, or innovation. The
  creator explains how to validate ideas using customer language and market
  data, how to run a smoke test, and how to build an early-stage MVP without
  wasting time or money.


  The second part focuses on customer acquisition methods suited for early-stage
  founders with no audience. You'll learn the difference between outbound and
  inbound strategies, the advantages of short-form content, how paid ads can
  deliver structured feedback on your market, and when SEO might make sense.
  Everything is grounded in real-world examples like the breastfeeding niche,
  and the bakery metaphor is used to explain the logic of entering competitive
  markets with thoughtful positioning.


  Ideal for indie SaaS founders, technical builders, or marketers looking to
  improve idea validation and early traction.
