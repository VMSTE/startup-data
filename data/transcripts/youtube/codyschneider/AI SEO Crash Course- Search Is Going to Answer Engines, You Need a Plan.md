  🔑 Key Takeaways

  AI Search Growth: ChatGPT’s web search launch in October 2024 drove mobile app
  downloads and referrals to unprecedented levels.


  Answer Engine vs. Chat: Unlike standalone LLM chat, AI answer engines scrape
  the open web then reason over that data—offering up-to-date, citation-backed
  responses.


  High-Impact Verticals: Long purchase-decision cycles (consumer electronics,
  auto, healthcare) and tech-savvy buyers see 3–4× month-over-month referral
  growth.


  Bot-First Attribution: Brands must track both AI crawler visits and human
  referrals in GA4 (or Amplitude) to measure AI search impact.


  Tactical SEO for AI:


  Build comparative articles (X vs. Y) with metadata optimized to surface in AI
  answers


  Leverage category-specific sources (Wikipedia, Reddit, niche publications)
  uncovered via Profound


  Create highly structured, markdown-style content (no JS/images) for fast
  crawler reasoning


  Future Outlook: Expect “llms.txt” and bot-first HTML feeds to become standard
  for AI agents. Brands will need dedicated AI visibility teams, akin to early
  social-media or SEO departments.
