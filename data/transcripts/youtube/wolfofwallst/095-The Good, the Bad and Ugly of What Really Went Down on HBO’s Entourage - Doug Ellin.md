  beloved series – Entourage – which became a cultural phenomenon and industry
  touchstone. The HBO show ran for eight seasons and Ellin made an eight-figure
  pact with HBO to continue producing television.

  brands – among them: Apple, Cadillac, Ferrari, Johnnie Walker, and Tequila
  Avión.

  Awards, three PGA Awards and a BAFTA, and has won a BAFTA and a PGA award.
