  In this tip, we go over how to make cold call intros to B2B prospects in the
  21st century. We don't​ word vomit, ask how their day is going, or bullshit
  them. We get right to the point. Wouldn't you respect that as a business
  person? 


  Respect your prospect by matching their tone (emotional attunement) and time,
  and manage the conversation so you move them along right into a question to
  get them talking. The faster you can get them to chat about their experience
  with a similar  product or service you are offering, the better chance you
  have of pitching your business.
