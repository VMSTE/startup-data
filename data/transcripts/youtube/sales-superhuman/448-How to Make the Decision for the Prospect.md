description: >-
  In this video Ryan explains how to make the decision for the prospect when it
  comes to setting an appointment.  If the prospect has already agreed to an
  email or call back, we can't expect them to know exactly when they'll be free
  for an appointment.  After all, decision makers tend to be busy people.  What
  we can do is suggest a time and date FOR them, this way it saves them the
  hassle of figuring it out for themselves, and in turn can lead to an
  appointment.
