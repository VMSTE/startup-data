description: >-
  Ryan explains why you shouldn't use gimmicks and fads when cold calling and
  what works better.  A pattern interrupt is an extremely effective technique
  when used properly. It can help change behaviors, assumptions, opinions, and
  decisions. This allows you to engage more effectively and create more
  meaningful interactions.  A good pattern interrupt in cold calling pushes
  people to not rely on their "go-to" responses. 


  But if you're not careful, it can easily turn into a gimmick or fad.  So how
  can you tell if what you're saying is gimmicky or not?  If you can say
  something that is repeatable to the masses and is genuine, that's okay.  The
  point is to always focus more on helping the prospect, so when we start
  focusing too much on executing the joke for example, that's when it becomes a
  gimmick and loses it's authenticity.  The prospect will see right through this
  which automatically loses their trust.  And as we know, the number one goal
  when speaking with prospects is gaining their trust.
