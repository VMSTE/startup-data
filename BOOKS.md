# Books

This is a reading list, not a library. Each entry is a book that showed up in the local cache we used while building this corpus. We publish the catalog and a handful of short quotes. We do not publish the files.

Links go to Wikipedia, Open Library, or the author's page. That is enough to find a legal copy. We do not link to pirate mirrors.

44 books. 22 quotes in `data/books/quotes.json`.

Machine-readable catalog: [`data/books/catalog.json`](data/books/catalog.json).

## Company building

### Zero to One (2014)

Peter Thiel, Blake Masters. Russian title in the cache: От нуля к единице.

Thiel's case for doing something new instead of scaling a copy. Useful next to the YC and a16z talks when you want the monopoly argument in one place.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_to_One) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Zero+to+One+Thiel).

Related talks in this repo: `ycombinator`, `paul-graham`, `a16z`.

### The Lean Startup (2011)

Eric Ries. Russian title in the cache: Бизнес с нуля.

Build-measure-learn as a loop. The default vocabulary for 'don't scale a ghost' in this corpus.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lean_Startup) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=The+Lean+Startup+Ries).

Related talks in this repo: `ycombinator`, `custdev-momtest`, `custdev-yc`.

### The Hard Thing About Hard Things (2014)

Ben Horowitz. Russian title in the cache: Легко не будет.

The ugly middle: firing, peacetime vs wartime CEO, not dying. Pair with First Round and SaaStr operating talks.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hard_Thing_About_Hard_Things) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Hard+Thing+About+Hard+Things+Horowitz).

Related talks in this repo: `firstround`, `a16z`, `saastr`.

### The Cold Start Problem (2021)

Andrew Chen. Russian title in the cache: Проблема холодного старта.

How a network gets its first users and then does not stall. The book behind the Andrew Chen / NFX / flywheel talks already in the transcript set.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=The+Cold+Start+Problem+Andrew+Chen) | [author](https://andrewchen.com/the-cold-start-problem/).

Related talks in this repo: `andrew-chen`, `nfx`, `flywheel-founders`.

### Blitzscaling (2018)

Reid Hoffman, Chris Yeh. Russian title in the cache: Блиц-масштабирование.

When to prioritize speed over efficiency. The flywheel-founders session already has the Stanford blitzscaling lectures; this is the book form.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blitzscaling) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Blitzscaling+Hoffman).

Related talks in this repo: `flywheel-founders`, `ycombinator`.

### Crossing the Chasm (1991)

Geoffrey A. Moore. Russian title in the cache: Преодоление пропасти.

Why a product that delights enthusiasts still dies in the mainstream. Still the map for B2B category passage.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossing_the_Chasm) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Crossing+the+Chasm+Moore).

Related talks in this repo: `april-dunford`, `lochhead`, `productled`.

### The Innovator's Dilemma (1997)

Clayton M. Christensen. Russian title in the cache: Дилемма инноватора.

Why good companies miss the next curve. Read it as a warning about listening only to today's best customers.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Innovator%27s_Dilemma) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Innovators+Dilemma+Christensen).

Related talks in this repo: `stratechery`, `a16z`.

## Growth and distribution

### Hooked (2014)

Nir Eyal. Russian title in the cache: На крючке.

Trigger-action-reward-investment. The habit loop that sits under a lot of consumer growth talk in the flywheel set.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hooked_(book)) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Hooked+Nir+Eyal).

Related talks in this repo: `flywheel-founders`, `brian-balfour`.

### Contagious (2013)

Jonah Berger. Russian title in the cache: Заразительный.

Why some things get talked about. STEPPS as a checklist, not as a growth department.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contagious:_Why_Things_Catch_On) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Contagious+Jonah+Berger).

Related talks in this repo: `flywheel-founders`, `garyvee`.

### Hacking Growth (2017)

Sean Ellis, Morgan Brown. Russian title in the cache: Взлом роста.

The growth-team version of build-measure-learn. Pair with Brian Balfour and Elena Verna talks.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Hacking+Growth+Sean+Ellis).

Related talks in this repo: `brian-balfour`, `elena-verna`, `flywheel-founders`.

### Platform Revolution (2016)

Geoffrey G. Parker, Marshall W. Van Alstyne, Sangeet Paul Choudary. Russian title in the cache: Революция платформ.

How multi-sided markets create and capture value. Complements Chen and NFX on the economics, not the cold-start tactics.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Platform+Revolution+Parker).

Related talks in this repo: `andrew-chen`, `nfx`, `stratechery`.

## Offers, sales, customer development

### $100M Offers (2021)

Alex Hormozi. Russian title in the cache: $100M Offers.

Value equation, grand-slam offer, guarantees. The densest offer-crafting book in the set; the Hormozi talks repeat it out loud.

Find it: [author](https://www.acquisition.com/training/offers) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=100M+Offers+Hormozi).

Related talks in this repo: `hormozi`.

### Influence (1984)

Robert Cialdini. Russian title in the cache: Психология влияния.

Reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity. The shared language under a lot of the sales-training slice.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influence:_The_Psychology_of_Persuasion) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Influence+Cialdini).

Related talks in this repo: `hormozi`, `girard`, `sales-scripter`.

### The Mom Test (2013)

Rob Fitzpatrick. Russian title in the cache: Спроси маму.

How to talk to users without fooling yourself. The custdev-momtest session is the talk form of this book.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mom_Test) | [author](https://www.momtestbook.com/).

Related talks in this repo: `custdev-momtest`, `custdev-yc`, `indie-hackers`.

### SPIN Selling (1988)

Neil Rackham. Russian title in the cache: SPIN-продажи.

Situation-Problem-Implication-Need-payoff. Older than MEDDICC, still the discovery spine a lot of B2B teams use.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPIN_selling) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=SPIN+Selling+Rackham).

Related talks in this repo: `meddicc`, `markroberge`, `sales-patrickdang`.

### The Challenger Sale (2011)

Matthew Dixon, Brent Adamson. Russian title in the cache: Чемпионы продаж.

Teach, tailor, take control. The alternative to relationship-only selling in complex B2B.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Challenger_Sale) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Challenger+Sale+Dixon).

Related talks in this repo: `meddicc`, `saastr`, `markroberge`.

### Fanatical Prospecting (2015)

Jeb Blount. Russian title in the cache: Фанатичные продажи.

Volume and cadence of outbound. Explains the sales-scripter / Cognism slice better than those clips explain themselves.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Fanatical+Prospecting+Blount).

Related talks in this repo: `sales-cognism`, `sales-scripter`, `aaronross`.

### How to Sell Anything to Anybody (1977)

Joe Girard. Russian title in the cache: Как продать что угодно кому угодно.

Law of 250, follow-up, treating every buyer as a future referral. The girard talks are this book spoken.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_to_Sell_Anything_to_Anybody) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=How+to+Sell+Anything+to+Anybody+Girard).

Related talks in this repo: `girard`.

## Strategy

### Good Strategy/Bad Strategy (2011)

Richard Rumelt. Russian title in the cache: Хорошая стратегия, плохая стратегия.

Diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent action. The cleanest test for whether a 'strategy' is just a list of goals.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Strategy/Bad_Strategy) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Good+Strategy+Bad+Strategy+Rumelt).

Related talks in this repo: `stratechery`, `lochhead`.

### Playing to Win (2013)

A.G. Lafley, Roger L. Martin. Russian title in the cache: Игра на победу.

The five strategy questions from P&G. Useful when a founder story never names a where-to-play choice.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Playing+to+Win+Lafley+Martin).

Related talks in this repo: `april-dunford`, `lochhead`.

### Blue Ocean Strategy (2005)

W. Chan Kim, Renée Mauborgne. Russian title in the cache: Стратегия голубого океана.

Value innovation and making a market instead of joining one. Overlap with Lochhead's category design talks.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Ocean_Strategy) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Blue+Ocean+Strategy+Kim).

Related talks in this repo: `lochhead`, `april-dunford`.

### Competitive Strategy (1980)

Michael E. Porter. Russian title in the cache: Конкурентная стратегия.

Five forces and generic strategies. The industry-structure baseline everything later either uses or argues with.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competitive_Strategy) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Competitive+Strategy+Porter).

Related talks in this repo: `stratechery`.

### Only the Paranoid Survive (1996)

Andrew S. Grove. Russian title in the cache: Выживают только параноики.

Strategic inflection points from the Intel years. How an operator notices the ground moving.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_the_Paranoid_Survive) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Only+the+Paranoid+Survive+Grove).

Related talks in this repo: `firstround`, `a16z`.

### The Art of Strategy (2008)

Avinash K. Dixit, Barry J. Nalebuff. Russian title in the cache: Стратегические игры.

Game theory for people who run businesses, not for exam questions. Complements Voss on the other side of a negotiation.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=The+Art+of+Strategy+Dixit+Nalebuff).

Related talks in this repo: `chris-voss`.

## Capital and institutions

### Venture Deals (2011)

Brad Feld, Jason Mendelson. Russian title in the cache: Venture Deals.

How a term sheet is built. Pair with the Brad Feld talks and 20VC fundraising interviews.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Venture+Deals+Feld+Mendelson) | [author](https://www.venturedeals.com/).

Related talks in this repo: `brad-feld`, `20vc`, `ycombinator`.

### The Intelligent Investor (1949)

Benjamin Graham. Russian title in the cache: Разумный инвестор.

Mr. Market and margin of safety. In this corpus it is background for how operators talk about not overpaying, not a trading manual.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Intelligent_Investor) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=The+Intelligent+Investor+Graham).

Related talks in this repo: `dalio`.

### The Essays of Warren Buffett (1997)

Warren Buffett, Lawrence A. Cunningham. Russian title in the cache: Эссе Уоррена Баффетта.

Capital allocation in plain language. Useful next to Thorndike's Outsiders.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Essays_of_Warren_Buffett) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Essays+of+Warren+Buffett+Cunningham).

### The Little Book That Beats the Market (2005)

Joel Greenblatt. Russian title in the cache: Маленькая книга победителя рынка акций.

A simple quality-plus-cheap screen. In the set as a foil to venture storytelling: some capital is allocated by formula.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Little_Book_That_Beats_the_Market) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Little+Book+That+Beats+the+Market+Greenblatt).

### Why Nations Fail (2012)

Daron Acemoglu, James A. Robinson. Russian title in the cache: Почему одни страны богатые, а другие бедные.

Inclusive vs extractive institutions. Context for CIS / low-trust market conversations, not a startup playbook.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Why_Nations_Fail) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Why+Nations+Fail+Acemoglu).

Related talks in this repo: `kunal-shah`, `yamalkevich`.

### The Mystery of Capital (2000)

Hernando de Soto. Russian title in the cache: Загадка капитала.

Why assets that people already use still cannot become capital. Same regional lens as Why Nations Fail.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mystery_of_Capital) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Mystery+of+Capital+de+Soto).

Related talks in this repo: `kunal-shah`.

## Organizations

### The Five Dysfunctions of a Team (2002)

Patrick Lencioni. Russian title in the cache: Пять пороков команды.

Trust, conflict, commitment, accountability, results. The Lencioni talks in the transcript set are this model repeated.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Five_Dysfunctions_of_a_Team) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Five+Dysfunctions+of+a+Team+Lencioni).

Related talks in this repo: `lencioni`.

### Measure What Matters (2018)

John Doerr. Russian title in the cache: Измеряйте самое важное.

OKRs as Intel and Google used them. How a company says what this quarter is for.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_What_Matters) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Measure+What+Matters+Doerr).

Related talks in this repo: `ycombinator`, `firstround`.

### Principles (2017)

Ray Dalio. Russian title in the cache: Принципы. Жизнь и работа.

Idea meritocracy and pain-plus-reflection. The dalio talks are the short form.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principles_(book)) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Principles+Ray+Dalio).

Related talks in this repo: `dalio`.

### Team of Teams (2015)

Stanley McChrystal. Russian title in the cache: Команда команд.

Shared consciousness and empowered execution. How a large org still moves when the map changes weekly.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_of_Teams) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Team+of+Teams+McChrystal).

Related talks in this repo: `lencioni`.

### Turn the Ship Around! (2012)

L. David Marquet. Russian title in the cache: Разверните ваш корабль.

Leader-leader instead of leader-follower. Intent-based execution from a submarine, mapped onto company teams.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turn_the_Ship_Around!) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Turn+the+Ship+Around+Marquet).

Related talks in this repo: `lencioni`.

### No Rules Rules (2020)

Reed Hastings, Erin Meyer. Russian title in the cache: Никаких правил.

Density of talent, candor, and the keeper test. One company's culture written as an operating system.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Rules_Rules) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=No+Rules+Rules+Hastings).

Related talks in this repo: `flywheel-founders`.

### The Toyota Way (2004)

Jeffrey K. Liker. Russian title in the cache: Дао Toyota.

The 14 principles behind Toyota production. Operations craft, not a startup origin story.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Toyota_Way) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=The+Toyota+Way+Liker).

### The Outsiders (2012)

William N. Thorndike. Russian title in the cache: Аутсайдеры / Правила лучших CEO.

Eight CEOs judged by capital allocation, not charisma. A different success metric than blitzscaling.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=The+Outsiders+Thorndike).

## Founder books and adjacent

### Shoe Dog (2016)

Phil Knight. Russian title in the cache: Продавец обуви.

Nike's early years as a cash and distribution problem. A founder memoir that is more useful on working capital than on slogans.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoe_Dog) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Shoe+Dog+Phil+Knight).

Related talks in this repo: `acquired`.

### Alibaba's World (2015)

Porter Erisman. Russian title in the cache: Alibaba. История мирового восхождения от первого лица.

An insider account of Alibaba before the IPO. Regional case, not a US SaaS playbook.

Find it: [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Alibaba%27s+World+Erisman).

Related talks in this repo: `acquired`.

### The Design of Everyday Things (1988)

Don Norman. Russian title in the cache: Дизайн привычных вещей.

Affordances, signifiers, error. Why a product feels obvious or hostile, independent of growth loops.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Design_of_Everyday_Things) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Design+of+Everyday+Things+Norman).

Related talks in this repo: `productled`.

### Thinking in Bets (2018)

Annie Duke. Russian title in the cache: Принцип ставок.

Resulting vs decision quality. A founder-facing way to talk about bets without pretending the outcome was knowable.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_in_Bets) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Thinking+in+Bets+Annie+Duke).

Related talks in this repo: `dalio`.

### Predictably Irrational (2008)

Dan Ariely. Russian title in the cache: Предсказуемая иррациональность.

How people misjudge price, relativity, and free. Background for offer and pricing talk.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predictably_Irrational) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Predictably+Irrational+Ariely).

Related talks in this repo: `hormozi`, `profitwell`.

### Antifragile (2012)

Nassim Nicholas Taleb. Russian title in the cache: Антихрупкость.

Systems that gain from disorder. In this corpus it is a lens on optionality, not a market-timing guide.

Find it: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antifragile) | [open library](https://openlibrary.org/search?q=Antifragile+Taleb).

Related talks in this repo: `dalio`.

## What we left out

The same cache had psychotherapy novels (Irvin Yalom), Jordan Peterson, and a few other titles that are not startup or business strategy. They stay out of this list.
